# Instruction for the progress fixture - one of the three stops, category (c)

Built by unit 081 task 7 beside `WI-stop.md`, which raises category (a) only.
Identical in shape. The `WHY` raises **what the product promises the operator** -
ARBITER.md section 6's third category.

**It names a FACT THE PRODUCT STATES about what was logged, heard or sent**, which
is what the owner's ruling of 2026-09-13 defines a promise to be. It deliberately
does NOT raise a hint, a label, a target, a card's next line or a name: that same
ruling says in terms those are never a promise and never a stop, and an arm that
raised one of them would be testing the wrong thing.

END-ARBITER-DECISION
