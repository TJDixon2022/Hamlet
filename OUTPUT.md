  tried. Pass `--decoder` and it stops guessing.
- **What WSJT-X's decode lines look like.** The parser accepts
  `HHMMSS  snr  dt  freqHz  ~  message` and **refuses anything else rather than
  skipping it**, printing the offending line verbatim. Send that line back and
  the parser is corrected against it. A parser that silently skipped what it did
  not recognise would write a fixture short by an unknown number of rows, and
  nothing downstream could tell.

**Nothing is ever substituted for WSJT-X** - not `decode_ft8.exe`, which is
`ft8_lib` and is the thing this project measures itself against, and not Hamlet's
own decoder, which would make the fixture a measurement of Hamlet against Hamlet.

is ever swapped, re-encoded, truncated or lost, the fixture beside it says so
loudly and the test suite goes red. It does not carry on and report a number that
looks exactly like a good one. That is the whole reason the hash is a full
SHA-256 of the file's bytes rather than CW's twelve-character fingerprint over
in-memory floats, which nothing recomputes and nobody outside this repository
