test(unit293): task 1 - what the transmit path does today with FT4 chosen

The trace, measured rather than described. With FT4 chosen and the dial on
14.080 MHz, SendMessage composes 12.64 s of FT8 tones (79 symbols, 8 tones at
6.25 Hz), arms them for a fifteen-second boundary, and the sequence keys, plays
and unkeys - telling the operator it had sent. Nothing between the click and
the keying frame notices that the operator asked for FT4.

The fit guard is FT8's in four places: 0.5 s into a 15 s slot leaves 14.5 s and
12.64 s fits. On FT4's 7.5 s slot the same offset leaves 7.0 s, which holds
FT4's 5.04 s with 1.96 s to spare and does not hold 12.64 s at all.

The right-click menu offers 3 shapes on an FT4 row against 5 on an FT8 row -
Report and RogerAndReport are absent because every FT4 row's ratio is null, and
the absence is said out loud in Ft8SendMenu.Absent.

Ft8ArmedSend.Arm has exactly one caller in src/.
