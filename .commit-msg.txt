test(unit293): task 5 - criterion 3, one click one FT4 transmission through the abort

One click, one transmission, asserted four ways on FT4: nothing but an arm arms;
a second arm replaces rather than adds; four FT4 boundaries after one arm produce
exactly one keying, counted on the bytes; and a decode, four ticks and a
countdown read to zero arm nothing at all, with the wire literally empty.

The abort fires on the FT4 path and it is the same abort. The sink is driven into
its failure path with 5.04 s of FT4 tones; the run comes back AudioFailed, carries
the AbortRecord rather than swallowing it, reports CameOutOfTransmit = TheAbort
and RadioIsInReceive = true, and the wire reads key-on, FE FE 94 E0 17 FF FD, then
1C 00 00 - the same three frames in the same order an FT8 failure produces.
TransmitAbort needed nothing and nothing in it changed. Its protocol-neutrality is
asserted rather than assumed: the file is read and contains no await, no async, no
Task, no lock, no WriteAsync, and no Ft4, Ft8 or Slot.

The stop takes an FT4 send off the air: it unarms, it fires the abort at the port,
and Ft8StopResult.Outcome names which of the six happened -
UnarmedAndToldTheRadio, distinguished from NothingToStop at an idle radio.

A refusal keys nothing at all. A licence refusal and a fit refusal each leave the
wire empty rather than writing a key-on followed by an abort, and both report
NothingWasKeyed. The third refusal on the path, a message no receiver could read
back, is in the application before the arm and is asserted there.

A boundary that has gone by discards an FT4 send rather than sending it late,
which on FT4 is a whole slot's error.
