feat(unit293): task 2 - Ft4Composer, beside Ft8Composer and not inside it

The words-to-audio half of criterion 3. Both composers are now faces onto a
shared DigitalComposer that holds the packing, the round trip, the five refusals
and the drive; what each face supplies is a ComposeGeometry - four constants and
three port calls, read off Ft8Sharp and typed nowhere in this assembly.

The seam was chosen this way because the two honest alternatives were one
implementation taking the modulation as a value, or two copies of a file. The
copy is what the port's whole argument is against: a second packer beside the
first is a second thing to drift, and the drift would be a message that says one
thing on FT8 and another on FT4.

Ft4SymbolEncoder.Encode and Ft4Waveform.Synthesize are called and nothing in
them is changed. The audio is asserted against the port sample for sample -
60480 samples, 105 symbols - so this seam cannot drift from what unit 289
nailed to gen_ft8 -ft4.

All five refusals are sentences on FT4 and none is an exception. The Nyquist
arithmetic is FT4's own: at tone 0 = 5950 Hz FT4's top tone is 6012.5 Hz and
refuses, FT8's is 5993.75 Hz and composes.

FT8 is untouched and it is asserted rather than described: ComposeSignal
produces the same array Ft8Waveform.Synthesize does for the same bits, and 53
existing FT8 composer tests are green.
