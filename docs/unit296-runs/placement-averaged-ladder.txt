WHAT THE CURVE SAYS. Down to -13 dB the placement costs almost nothing at the shipping
grid: 396 of 400 placement-averaged, with the worst of the twenty-five cells at 15 of 16.
The knee is between -13 and -15 dB and it is sharp - 99.0% to 47.0% in two decibels - and
below -15 the mode is gone, 2 of 400 at -17 and nothing at all below that. On grid the
knee is one rung further down: 62% at -15 against the lattice's 47%, so what remains of
the placement penalty lives entirely in that one rung.

AGAINST THE ON-GRID LADDER AT HEAD'S GRID, which is what the phase has been quoting.
Ft4SensitivityLadderTests read 106 of 106 at 0, -5, -10 and -13, 49 of 106 at -15, and 0
at -17, -19 and -21. This ladder's on-grid column reproduces that shape at a sixth of the
trials: 100% down to -13, 62% at -15, 0 below. The difference between the two columns HERE
is the placement and nothing else, because both are drawn in the same run at the same grid
on the same messages with the same noise draws.

WHAT THIS IS NOT. Everything Ft4SensitivityLadderTests disclaims applies unchanged: not a
step 6 result, not comparable with any published FT4 threshold, a curve over audio this
library synthesized itself - no fading, no drift, no neighbours, one signal in an empty
slot, one process, one seed. What it adds is the one axis that ladder had no way to vary.
