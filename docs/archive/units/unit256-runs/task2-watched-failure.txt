================================================================================
WHY THIS IS THE DEFECT WORTH WATCHING, AND NOT A TYPO
================================================================================

READ THE THREE THINGS THE DEFECTIVE TABLE SAYS.

1. EVERY WIDTH IS NEGATIVE. The band is inverted on every single row - its
   "optimistic" end sits at a WORSE ratio than its "pessimistic" one. An inverted
   interval contains nothing at all, and the containment check is what said so.

2. Ft8Sharp ON GRID READS -19.53 TO -19.56, A BAND 0.021 dB WIDE. The two rungs
   support 0.162 dB, EIGHT TIMES WIDER. Published, that table would have claimed a
   precision of two hundredths of a decibel from 306 trials, and the very next
   sentence anybody wrote would have compared it with another decoder's figure.
   CLAUDE.md 0.0 forbids exactly that comparison and the band exists to stop it.

3. SHIPPING ON GRID READS "-19.81 dB to -20.02 dB" AND CLAIMS TO BE CLOSED ON BOTH
   SIDES. It is not closed. Its -20 dB Wilson upper bound is 50.700 per cent, still
   above 50, so the optimistic curve never crosses inside the bracket at all and
   that side is OPEN. The defective pairing manufactured a bound where the
   measurement has none - which is an extrapolation in all but name, and unit 255's
   ruling 3 forbids it.

THE ARITHMETIC BEHIND IT. Write the crossing's position in the bracket as

    t(a, b) = (a - 50) / (a - b)

with a the upper rung's rate and b the lower rung's. Both partial derivatives are
positive:

    dt/da = (50 - b) / (a - b)^2 > 0
    dt/db = (a - 50) / (a - b)^2 > 0

so t is increasing in BOTH arguments. t(hi, hi) is therefore the extreme optimistic
end and t(lo, lo) the extreme pessimistic one, and any mixed pairing lands strictly
inside - which is why the wrong pairing does not merely narrow the band, it inverts
it. On SHIPPING on grid:

  hi(-19)=94.940 against lo(-20)=39.618 gives -19.812 dB - WORSE than the point
    crossing -19.897 dB it is meant to bracket
  lo(-19)=88.974 against hi(-20)=50.700 gives -20.018 dB - outside the bracket
    entirely
