# PHASE_GOAL.md

**Phase 10**

## The goal

**80% correct translation on a single clear CW signal.**

## What that means

**Correct** is measured against what was actually sent, character by character, by
edit distance. Not elements per character, not the fraction of letters that are E
or T, not a sensitivity sweep. Those are proxies and every one of them can improve
while the text stays unreadable.

**A single clear signal** is one sender, audible to the operator, in a passband
that is not being shared. It is not a weak signal and it is not a crowded band.
Those are worth solving and they are not this phase.

**80%** is four characters in five. That is readable — the errors are the kind an
operator fills in from context.

## What this phase does not claim

It does not claim a crowded passband, a weak signal, or perfect word spacing.
A pipeline measured on 2026-08-22 read `NACKETY` for `PACKET` and `TELEWRINTER`
for `TELEPRINTER` while reading the bulletin. Some residual errors are
segmentation and some are the air.

## The measurement problem, stated

**This phase cannot be scored until a capture has an answer key.**

Three adjudicated fragments exist — `N4L` (HM-DEC-144), `VA3VRR` (HM-DEC-145),
`AA4MP/4 QNIK` (HM-DEC-126). ARLP034 was never published; the ARRL propagation
archive stops at ARLP033, 14 August 2026.

**Until one capture is adjudicated end to end, every unit in this phase is
measured by proxy and the phase's own number cannot be stated.**
