1. Where our decoder assigns a character's class.
   src/Hamlet.RadioEngine/Cw/CwProbabilisticStream.cs:750 `Character(...)`:
     Unreadable where the alphabet does not know the pattern;
     High (sure) where GapsFitTheUnit(character, result, window) holds (every inner gap within
       InnerGapEdge units of the path's unit - HM-REQ-015, unit 445);
     Low (dim) otherwise.
   It is called at CwProbabilisticStream.cs:609, which raises CharacterSettled, and for the
   leading edge (LeadingEdgeChanged, line 621). `CwProbabilisticDecoder` itself assigns no class;
   the stream that hosts it does. The metrics read the class as CwSymbol.Of does
   (tests CwMetrics.cs:48): High = Sure, Low = NotSure, Unreadable = Placeholder.

2. Where CwPitchProof and CwSpeedProof are computed, and whether both are known at emission.
   CwPitchProof: CwToneTracker.PitchProof (CwToneTracker.cs:478), a live property:
     None where no pitch was measured (_reportedHz NaN); Proved where the current survey verdict's
     keyed candidate is at exactly the reported pitch; Hypothesis otherwise.
   CwSpeedProof: CwDecoder.SpeedProof (CwDecoder.cs:696), a live property:
     None where the stream's last read has no text or no speed; Proved where WordsPerMinute names
     a number (not re-acquiring after a follow, 6 to 48 WPM), the stream's unit was measured
     (estimator's dit or the marks' overrule, not the grid's winner), the tracker found keying in
     the last six surveys (KeyingRecently), and that keying was within half the mixdown filter of
     the pitch the stream reads; Hypothesis otherwise.
   BOTH ARE KNOWN AT THE MOMENT A CHARACTER IS EMITTED. Neither is computed after the fact for the
   report: both are properties of current state, read on demand. CwDecoder hosts the tracker and
   the stream; the stream raises CharacterSettled from inside CwProbabilisticStream.Process
   (CwDecoder.Step, CwDecoder.cs:899), after it has set Last and UnitWasMeasured for this read
   (CwProbabilisticStream.cs:533-534, before :609), and after the tracker has processed the same
   hop (CwDecoder.cs:853). So in CwDecoder's CharacterSettled and LeadingEdgeChanged handlers
   (CwDecoder.cs:129 and :137), _tracker.PitchProof and SpeedProof are the states at emission.
   The stream cannot see them (it holds no tracker), so the class cannot be gated where it is
   assigned without handing the stream the tracker. The gate therefore sits in CwDecoder, where
   the proof states are first known together, on our character before it meets the arbiter.

   What "at emission" means here: a character settles after the decision delay, so its audio (At)
   is earlier than the moment it is emitted. The states read are those at the emission moment,
   which is what DECIDED (3) says ("at its emission").

3. Departures of the proof states from their RESOLVED lines (reported, not repaired).
   (a) Pitch. RESOLVED: "proved when the independent survey admits keying at that pitch AND at
       least one whole character has resolved at it; hypothesis when a tone is tracked but keying
       is not admitted or no character has resolved". The tree's PitchProof has the first half
       (the current keyed verdict is at the reported pitch) and NOT the second: no character need
       have resolved at the pitch for Proved. Departure: the tree can say Proved before any
       character has resolved. The gate reads the tree as it stands, so on this clause's account
       it dims fewer letters than the RESOLVED line would, never more. Recorded.
   (b) Speed. RESOLVED: "proved only while characters resolve at it with the marks' unit within
       10 percent of the path's (HM-REQ-031); when the tone stops or the pitch changes it drops to
       hypothesis". The tree's SpeedProof has "the tone stops" (KeyingRecently, six surveys) and
       "the pitch changes" (SpeedIsReacquiring after a follow of half the filter or more, and the
       last keying within half the filter of the stream's pitch). It has no explicit test that
       the marks' unit is within 10 percent of the path's: "measured" is the estimator's dit or
       the marks' overrule rather than the grid's winner. Unit 451's trace found 0 of 24418 proved
       synthetic hops more than 10 percent off the constructed speed, so on the synthetic set the
       two agree in effect; on real audio the 10 percent clause is not checked. Departure recorded.
   (c) The tree's pitch None is "no pitch measured" (_reportedHz NaN) where RESOLVED says "no tone
       tracked". The tracker always points somewhere (a bank centre) and reports no measured pitch
       until the survey admits keying, so the two coincide in practice. Not a departure in effect.

4. How the arbiter takes our class (CwDecoder.Arbitrated, CwArbiter.Decide).
   The harness every metric here is measured through (TheRequirementsAreMeasuredTests,
   TheSeventeenThirtySevenCaptureTests.Settle, the floors) builds CwDecoder without the second
   reader, so Arbitrated returns our character unchanged: the emitted transcript there IS our
   class. The gate's effect on every R78 figure is therefore exactly the gate.
   With the second reader on (the application, and TheArbitrationEarnsItsPlaceTests part (b)),
   the live switch is Arbitrate (CwSwitchTable "real HF, all"). CwArbiter.Decide:
     no partner: ours as it is (a gated letter stays dim);
     OursAlone: ours as it is;
     only one votes / neither: the voter's reading at its own class - where only the port votes,
       the port's class is emitted and our gate does not reach it;
     both vote and agree: the more confident decoder's class - where the port's p beats ours, the
       port's class is emitted, which can put sure back on a letter our gate dimmed;
     both vote and tie: dim;
     both vote and differ: the higher p at its own class.
   So in the application the gate is the gate on OUR reading; a port reading of the same span that
   wins the arbitration can still reach the screen sure while acquiring. This is the arbiter as
   ruled (HM-REQ-121 to 127, 466), untouched here (section 7 parks every switch and the vote
   table). The live vote at "real HF, all" is read from CwVoteTable.Live.

   after-figure exists).

Part 3 - the rule, registered before any after-figure exists (DECIDED (3), as the tree implements it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where: CwDecoder (src/Hamlet.RadioEngine/Cw/CwDecoder.cs), in the two handlers that take our
stream's characters - CharacterSettled (before Arbitrated) and LeadingEdgeChanged (before the edge
is arbitrated or raised). That is where the pitch's and the speed's proof states are first known
together (Part 1, item 2); the stream that assigns the class holds no tracker.

Rule: a character whose Confidence is High is emitted with Confidence Low when, at that moment,
_tracker.PitchProof is not CwPitchProof.Proved or SpeedProof is not CwSpeedProof.Proved.
- Word gaps, Low and Unreadable characters pass unchanged. No letter changes, none is dropped,
  none goes below dim, none is raised, nothing is promoted after the fact.
- No constant, threshold, grace period or character count. Both states are read as they stand;
  nothing about how either is computed changes (R75, R76, section 7).
- One form only. If refused, no second form is tried (V-14).
- The port (Cw/Second/), the arbiter, the switch and vote tables are not touched; with the second
  reader on, the arbiter receives our gated character as Part 1, item 4 describes.

The test: NoSureLetterWhileAcquiringTests, naming HM-REQ-102 and HM-REQ-010, on a generated case
with its key exact by construction - watched red at HEAD first.

MET-COVERAGE falling refuses (DECIDED (4)).
