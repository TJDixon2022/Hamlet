1. WHAT CwFixtureGenerator DOES, STAGE BY STAGE
-----------------------------------------------
sample rate    SampleRate = 8000 (line 92). Every output is 8000 samples a second.

keying         KeyEdges (365-465) turns the recipe's own dit, dah, element, character and word
envelope       gaps into a list of key-change instants. Lead-in 1.0 s (LeadInSeconds, 140) plus any
               preamble before the first mark; tail 1.5 s (TailSeconds, 143) after the last edge.
               A caret joins letters into a prosign with an element gap (417-436).
edge shaping   Gate (636-641): raised cosine, 5 ms rise and 5 ms fall (EdgeSeconds, 137), inside
               each element - the rise starts on the leading edge, the fall ends on the trailing
               edge, so the edge instants are exact and the half-amplitude mark is 5 ms short.

tone           RenderKeying (566-633): sin(phase), phase accumulated per sample from
               ToneHz + DriftHz * sin(2 pi t / 10 s) (585-586) + DriftHzPerSecond * t (588-591);
               added as amplitude * gate * fade on top of the noise already in the buffer (631).
               The recipe default is 615 Hz drifting +/- 3 Hz.

ShapedNoise    (477-534) xorshift32 uniforms (481-487), Box-Muller to unit-variance Gaussian white
               noise (489-503); three cascaded RBJ bandpass biquads, constant-0-dB-peak form
               (b0 = alpha, b2 = -alpha), centre sqrt(350 * 870) = 551.8 Hz, Q = 551.8 / 520 = 1.061
               (508-514, Bandpass 537-563); the shaped part scaled to RMS 0.02 exactly (517-525);
               the unshaped white added underneath at 0.02 * 10^(-30/20) (526-531), the "30 dB
               skirt". It returns 0.02 (533): the RMS of the shaped part alone.
  measured     total RMS 0.02002 (-33.97 dBFS) - the skirt adds 0.1 % of power. The skirt is 30 dB
               down in TOTAL power but 41.8 to 42.5 dB below the peak in DENSITY (at 2000 Hz and
               100 Hz), because it is spread over 4000 Hz and the band over about 300.
               -3 dB points of the measured density: 436 to 691 Hz, not 350 to 870.

SignalToNoiseDb  applied at 336 (Generate) and 273-274 (Together): tone amplitude =
               sqrt(2) * 0.02 * 10^(snr/20), so tone RMS over the shaped band's RMS is the recipe's
               figure. That is an SNR in the band's own equivalent bandwidth - neither 2500 Hz nor
               the 520 Hz between the named edges. Confirms the instruction: a finding, not
               repaired here (CW_SPEC 8.1 re-labels every such figure).

QSB            (612-629) a deterministic sinusoid cos(2 pi QsbHz t), depth split +/- depth/2 dB
               about the stated level, on the tone only. One path, no delay, no random process -

4. THE OPEN DEFINITIONS, READ UNDER R85
---------------------------------------
Sources: CW_SPEC.md 8.1, 8.2, 9.1; C. C. Watterson, J. R. Juroshek, W. D. Bensema, "Experimental
confirmation of an HF channel model", IEEE Trans. Commun. Technol. COM-18(6), Dec 1970;
CCIR Rec. 520 (now ITU-R F.520) and ITU-R F.1487. None of the three published sources is vendored
(CW_SPEC 12 item 2); they are read from their published descriptions, not from a file in the tree.

 a. SPREAD. The author's reading is taken: the frequency spread is 2 sigma, twice the standard
    deviation of each path's Gaussian Doppler power spectrum S(f) ~ exp(-f^2 / (2 sigma^2)), with
    no frequency shift. Watterson et al. define the spread as 2 sigma of the Gaussian spectrum and
    CCIR 520 / F.1487 tabulate "frequency spread" on that definition. So sigma = spread / 2.
    Measured as 2 sqrt(second moment) of the tap's power spectrum.

 b. WHERE THE NOISE ENTERS. The author's reading is taken: after the fading. The Watterson model
    is a tapped delay line whose taps fade; the additive noise is the receiver's and the band's,
    not the path's, and CW_SPEC 8.2 measures it in the receiver passband. So the noise is never
    faded, and the SNR is the unfaded mean signal power against the noise. "Two paths of equal
    power" (9.1) is read as two paths whose SUM is the unfaded power, each carrying half: with
    each gk at unit power, hk = gk / sqrt(2). Without that the faded signal would sit 3 dB above
    the SNR it is labelled with.
    SNR_2500 = S / (N0(f0) * 2500 Hz), S the tone's mean power in a mark (A^2 / 2 unfaded; the
    long-run mean when faded), N0(f0) the band's one-sided density beside the tone.

 c. TX-ITU IN THE TREE. The recipe defaults are NOT TX-ITU (105 / 283 / 65 / 130 / 280 ms is
    013347's fist). SyntheticCq.Recipe(wpm, snr, seed) at its default characterGapUnits of 3 IS
    exactly 1:3:1:3:7 at the key-change instants: dit 1200/wpm, dah 3, element gap 1, character
    gap 3, word gap 7 (SyntheticCq.cs 71-87). It is named TX-ITU. Two things ride on it that are
    not the keying ratio: the 5 ms raised-cosine edges inside each element (the emission's rise
    and fall, CW_SPEC 7), and the recipe's default +/- 3 Hz drift. The channel layer keys its
    envelope through the same KeyEdges and Gate and holds the pitch fixed (no drift), because the
    channel's construction has one f0.
