You are judging one thing and nothing else.

Below is section 4 of a work unit's report. Its heading is
"What's blocking us". The convention is that a unit writes here
any question that needs a ruling from the owner - and that an
empty section 4 is a real answer meaning nothing is blocked.

Many units write a sentence SAYING nothing is blocking rather
than leaving it blank. THAT IS NOT A RULING REQUEST. Neither is
a note, an observation, a thing reported for the record, or a
recommendation the unit has already acted on.

A RULING IS WANTED only where the text asks the owner to decide
something, or says work is stopped until he does.

WHERE A RULING IS WANTED, JUDGE ONE MORE THING, AND IT IS THE
POINT OF THIS CALL. A ruling that forecloses the whole step is
different from one that stops a single exit criterion while other
work on the same step remains open.

Read PHASE_PLAN.md for this step exit criteria and
PHASE_OUTCOME.md for what has already been done. Then decide
whether ANY work on this step could still proceed without the
owner answer. The step being worked is step 5.

Halting a night for a question that forecloses nothing is the
failure this judgment exists to prevent. Building on an assumption
the owner would have rejected is the other one. Weigh both and
reason it out. Do not count the rulings - judge what they block.

Answer with exactly two lines and nothing else:

VERDICT: blocking
WHY: one sentence, plain text, no punctuation beyond commas and full stops

or

VERDICT: banked
WHY: one sentence, plain text, no punctuation beyond commas and full stops

or

VERDICT: none
WHY: one sentence, plain text, no punctuation beyond commas and full stops

blocking means a ruling is wanted and NO work on this step can
