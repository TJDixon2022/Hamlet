MECHANISMS CITED
  (A) detection: hysteresis on the AGC-normalised magnitude, thresholds set between tracked floor and peak each
      decision sample - cw.cxx:610-623 (decayavg of noise_floor, agc_peak), 640-641 (CWupper, CWlower), 649-656.
  (B) noise-spike rule: a key-down shorter than half a dot length is thrown away, not classed - cw.cxx:515, 818-822.
  (C) element classing: a dot is anything up to two_dots, a dash anything longer - cw.cxx:847-855.
  (D) speed tracking: two_dots follows dot-dash and dash-dot pairs through a 16-sample moving average -
      cw.cxx:524-535 (update_tracking), 832-843.
  (E) gap handling: a character ends after 2 to 4 dot lengths of key-up, a word space after more than 4 -
      cw.cxx:880-914 (888 and 910).
  (F) overflow and no-match: more than 6 elements resets the character (858-864); a representation with no table
      entry prints CW_noise `*` (892-898), which is scored as a placeholder.
  (G) no refusal: nothing in the receive path declines to print on a weak or absent signal; whatever crosses CWupper
      is keyed (649), with the squelch off as fldigi's file benchmark runs it (benchmark.cxx:54).
