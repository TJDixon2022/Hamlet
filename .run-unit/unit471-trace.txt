===============================================================================

Part 1 - the code, read before any decode (HEAD 78f69b94, src identical to a2e07f6d)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. The path.
   Scored: TheRequirementsAreMeasuredTests drives `new CwDecoder(rate, pitch)` hop by hop (5 ms
   chunks) and flushes - real through TheSeventeenThirtySevenCaptureTests.Settle (CwDecoder at
   600 Hz), synthetic through its own Settle (CwDecoder at SyntheticCq.StartingPitchHz). The
   second reader is OFF, so CwDecoder.Arbitrated (CwDecoder.cs:215) returns our character
   unchanged: the scored transcript is CwProbabilisticStream's CharacterSettled, as the stream
   classed it. Not the whole-file decode (CwProbabilisticDecoder.Decode(MonoAudio)) and not the
   arbiter.
   CW tab: MainWindowViewModel.cs:11215 builds `new CwDecoder(rate, pitch, secondReader: true)`.
   The same CwProbabilisticStream settles the same characters; each then passes through
   CwArbiter.Decide against the port's reading of the same span (live switch Arbitrate), and the
   leading edge through ArbitratedEdge.
   How they differ: only the arbiter after the stream. Both paths share the stream and
   CwDecoder.Step, so a change inside the stream, or in CwDecoder before Arbitrated, acts the
   same on both (the arbiter can still put the port's reading on a span in the app, as it
   already does for every span). A change in the harness, or after the arbiter, would not.

2. The window (CwProbabilisticStream.cs).
   The stream keeps a rolling envelope of WindowSeconds = 12 s (2400 hops of 5 ms), mixed down
   hop by hop at whatever ToneHz CwDecoder.Step set for that hop (cs:885), through a trailing
   Hann integrator; it keeps NO raw audio beyond the integrator's own ring (_mixedI/_mixedQ,
   one integrator length).
   Every ReadEverySeconds = 0.5 s (100 hops) Read() decodes the whole window again from
   scratch, at the speed that read measures (estimator, or the marks' overrule, or the grid).
   The settled mark is _settledThrough (cs:736), the absolute end hop of the last settled
   character. The decision window is DecisionDelaySeconds = 1.0 s (_delayHops = 200 hops):
   a character settles at the first read where its end hop is more than 200 hops behind the
   newest hop (cs:567-613), so 1.0 to 1.5 s after its audio ends. In dits at the speed in
   force, 1.0 s is wpm/1.2 dits: 10 at 12 WPM, 15 at 18, 20.8 at 25.
   Inside the window (the leading edge, LeadingEdgeChanged): text, class, character boundaries
   and spaces can all change on the next read. Behind it: nothing. A character whose absolute
   end is at or before _settledThrough is never announced again (cs:596), and Restart keeps
   the settled mark ("NOTHING ALREADY SETTLED IS RETRACTED", cs:324).

3. The proof states.
   CwPitchProof: CwToneTracker.PitchProof (CwToneTracker.cs:478), live: None with no measured
   pitch, Proved when the survey's keyed candidate is at the reported pitch, else Hypothesis.
   CwSpeedProof: CwDecoder.SpeedProof (CwDecoder.cs:696), live, read from the stream's LAST
   read: Proved when WordsPerMinute names a number, the read's unit was measured, and keying at
   the stream's pitch was seen in the last six surveys; else Hypothesis, or None with no text.
   Relative to the settled mark: both are current state, known at every read. The stream
   settles inside Read() after setting Last (cs:533, :604), and CwDecoder's CharacterSettled
   handler runs synchronously inside that read, so the states read there (470's "at
   emission") are the states at the read that settles the letter.
   Consequence for the question "which acquiring letters are still unsettled when both first
   become proved": a letter emitted while acquiring in 470's sense had both states read at its
   own settling read, and they were not both proved there. So both can have been proved while
   it was still unsettled only if they were proved at an EARLIER read, after its audio ended
   and before it settled, and then fell back (the states flicker: KeyingRecently, the keyed
   verdict at the reported pitch, SpeedIsReacquiring). The fact measures exactly that, read
   by read; nothing is assumed.
   The speed half is already met by construction: at a read where SpeedProof is Proved, that
   same read decoded its whole window, the leading edge included, at result.WordsPerMinute,
   which IS the proved speed (SpeedProof reads Last). So a re-read at the proved speed differs
   from HEAD's own reading at that read only through the pitch the envelope was mixed at.

4. Re-reading.
   CwProbabilisticDecoder.Decode(envelope, toneHz, wpm, gapMilliseconds) (cs:699) decodes any
   envelope at an imposed speed with given gaps; IntegratorWindow/IntegratorTaper (cs:593,
   :618) are the stream's integrator. So a span can be decoded again at a given pitch and speed
   without touching anything settled - but only from raw audio, which the stream does not
   hold. Where it would sit: CwProbabilisticStream.Read, with a raw-audio ring the length of
   the window, fed the two proof states by CwDecoder.Step (the stream holds no tracker); at the
   first read where both are proved, the unsettled acquiring letters would take the reading of
   a decode of the re-mixed window at the proved pitch and speed, and settle as that reading.
   Nothing behind _settledThrough is touched.
   In the fact, the re-read is computed outside src with the same pieces: the window at that
   read re-mixed from the raw audio at the proved pitch through the stream's trailing
   integrator, decoded at the proved speed with the gap lengths that read used, and classed by
   the stream's own GapsFitTheUnit.

Part 3 - the rule, registered before any after-figure exists (DECIDED (3) and (4) as the tree
will implement them)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Files: src/Hamlet.RadioEngine/Cw/CwProbabilisticStream.cs (new ReadAgainAtProvedValues, a raw
audio ring beside the envelope, and the settling pass in Read), and
src/Hamlet.RadioEngine/Cw/CwDecoder.cs (Step: the call).

 1. The stream keeps the raw samples behind its envelope (the window plus one integrator
    length), cleared on Restart; nothing else about the window changes.
 2. CwDecoder.Step, after the stream has processed a hop on which it read, takes both proof
    states as the tree computes them (CwToneTracker.PitchProof, CwDecoder.SpeedProof). At a
    read where both are Proved and at the previous read they were not both Proved - the first
    proof after every letter emitted while acquiring - it calls
    CwProbabilisticStream.ReadAgainAtProvedValues(pitchHz: Tracker.ToneHz,
    wpm: Reading.WordsPerMinute), the values the tree reports with proof state proved at that
    moment (DECIDED (4)).
 3. ReadAgainAtProvedValues mixes the window's raw audio down again at that pitch through the
    stream's own trailing integrator, decodes it with CwProbabilisticDecoder.Decode at that
    speed and the gap lengths the read just used, and pins every letter of that decode whose
    end lies after the settled mark and at or before the previous read's newest hop (audio that
    arrived while acquiring), each classed by the stream's own Character/GapsFitTheUnit on the
    re-read window. The region it covers runs from the settled mark to that hop.
 4. When the settling pass reaches the region, a letter of the ordinary read whose end lies in
    the region, or whose span overlaps a pinned letter, is not settled; the pinned letters
    settle instead, in order, each when the ordinary pass settles past its end, and the mark
    moves as they do. Where the re-read has nothing, nothing settles. Spaces settle as the
    ordinary read places them. A later proof re-pins what is still unsettled.
 5. Nothing behind the settled mark is touched. The window, the delay, the read cadence and
    both proof states are as they are. No constant is added. The leading edge is left as the
    ordinary read offers it (provisional, as today).
 6. The change sits in the stream and in CwDecoder.Step, before the arbiter, so the path the
    harness scores and the path the CW tab reads settle the same letters.

What table 1 foretells, stated before the build: no wrong letter is reachable that the re-read
turns right on either set, and one right letter on each set is reachable that it turns wrong or
loses. The change is built and measured anyway, because table 1 is not empty (task 1 step 5),
and judged on what it measures, not on this forecast.
