WHY (D) AND NOT (A), IN ONE LINE: on 17:37 the missing dahs of R and D stand no higher than noise in our envelope

WHAT (D) IS IN THE PORT. On every key-up the port times the mark; a mark shorter than half its tracked dot is a
spike and never reaches the tracker (cw.cxx:515, 818-822); otherwise, when this mark and the one before it are a
dot-dash or dash-dot pair - one more than two and less than four times the other (831-843) - update_tracking
(524-535) takes their mean, which is two dots, into a 16-long moving average, two_dots (533), from which the receive
speed follows (sync_parameters, 466-516; the speed at 502). It is a speed with memory, fed only by pairs of marks.
Ours takes its speed afresh from each 12 s window's two short clusters (CwUnitEstimator.Measure), with an overrule to
the window's marks where they say the path runs slow (CwProbabilisticStream, 441), and no memory from read to read.
