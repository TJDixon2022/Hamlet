WHAT FLDIGI NEEDS BEFORE A FIRST ELEMENT TO READ IT, IN ITS OWN TIME CONSTANTS
  A settled AGC: agc_peak raised by earlier key-down at the signal's own level, until noise over
  agc_peak sits under CWlower in a gap. It rises only during marks, with the attack weight 200
  decision samples (0.4 s). Here the first dit and dah, about 0.27 s of mark, took it from 0.029
  to 0.13 (4.6 times the noise's level), and from then on no gap raised a noise event. The level
  relation alone does not supply it: after long noise the thresholds are relative to agc_peak and
  settle inside the noise at any SNR, so the pre-dit noise key-down (45% of the time here) does
  not depend on the level. A quiet interval does not supply it either: the case is never digital
  silence (V-06), and noise is what keys the detector. So the element fldigi can read is one that
  follows keying at the same level within a few of its decay constants (sig_avg and a falling
  agc_peak decay by the weight 1000, 2.0 s), not one that follows seconds of noise alone.

  This is the loss 456's first look saw on every send ("loses the start of every send"), and the
  squelch (parked, status.cxx not in the tree) is not what turns it: task 2's verdict does not
  rest on the squelch, so the parked item is not re-opened.
