**NONE OF THEM SAYS HE FOLLOWED AND NONE SAYS HE REFUSED** (section 0.0,
decision BP), asserted by sweep: an RSID burst carries a mode code and no
callsign, so what the card reports is a code at a place.

THE WINDOW IS THE PRODUCT, NEVER SECONDS (section 3.2). PatienceCharacters 32
times 16/500's seconds per character = 16.384 s of patience, times the stated
whole factor 2 = 32.768 s. The test asserts the product against the file's
own windowSeconds and asserts the factor is 2; the number 32.768 is written
nowhere in src/.

WHAT COUNTS AS AN ANNOUNCEMENT, AND WHAT DOES NOT. An Olivia 16/500 code
whose center is within 16/500's own half width of the new center - +/-234.375
Hz from the format file, which is the plan's "about 250 Hz" arithmetic - heard
inside the window. Measured: a real 16/500 send 900 Hz away does not count,
and a real 8/250 send exactly on the new center does not count. Both leave
the card saying it is still waiting.

DECISION BQ, AND WHY THE DETECTION RATHER THAN THE CHANNEL. The follow check
reads HearRsid's detections. RsidDetection carries the code and the center at
the moment the burst ended, which is what R29 asks about; an Olivia channel
opens only once blocks have synchronised, and it can be opened by the blind
search, which is not an announcement at all. **The lift is one inch and no
more**: no detection sets the mode, the tab, the dial or a variant, and
R27's across-tab switch stays parked. The whole of it is one card sentence.

NOTHING TRANSMITS ON A DETECTION (section 0.2), asserted. His whole
transmission - the burst and 23 characters of 16/500 - is fed to the real
tick after the move: one play, one transmission record, one psk31_send_composed,
one keyed stage, and the port frame count unchanged at 2 before and after the
detection.

DECISION BR, THE CARD SURVIVES THE MOVE. Proved with real audio rather than
by injection: his old channel at the calling center goes, his new one opens
at 2000.0 Hz at 16/500 found by rsid with 23 characters read, and it is the
same one card for the same station - turn "Your turn", offering Report,
variant now 16/500, its conversation still holding the answer he sent at the
old place. What that took is in task 3's commit: the card was never in danger
(it is keyed by callsign), but the conversation it is read from is one
