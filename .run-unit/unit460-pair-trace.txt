HOW IT WAS READ
- The printer is WhereThePairSpeedMovedTheUnitTests.EveryReadOnTheNamedRecordings. It needs no src change.
  It drives our decoder hop by hop as the metrics do, keeping every read of the stream: 399 reads over
  the seven recordings, one every 0.5 s over a 12 s window. At each read it applies 6a0b65a1's
  CwUnitEstimator.PairUnit, copied line for line, and keeps every pair it took and every mark it passed
  over as a spike. Every settled letter is aligned to its key by TheRequirementsAreMeasuredTests.Measure.
- It was run twice, at HEAD (.run-unit/unit460-pair-head.txt) and with the diff applied
  (.run-unit/unit460-pair-diff.txt), and the two runs were joined read by read
  (.run-unit/unit460-pairjoin.sh).
- SAMENESS: 399 of 399. On every read, the stream's own speed with the diff applied equals what the copied
  rule predicts: the pair speed where the rule moves, and HEAD's speed where it does not. So every pair
  printed below is the one the refused change used.
- UNIT BEFORE is the path's unit at HEAD, after the marks' overrule. UNIT AFTER is the stream's unit with
  the diff. MARKS IMPLY is CwUnitEstimator.MarkUnit over the window's marks.
- KEY UNIT is the median, over the letters either run settled sure and right inside the window, of each
  letter's own span (first mark to last, from the read it settled at) over the units of its pattern
  (dit and inner gap 1, dah 3). A letter the key confirms was read element for element, so its span is
  the sender's timing whatever speed the path had. EVERY KEY IS INFERRED (V-13). Where the window holds
  no such letter, the direction is not given.
- Samples are at 48000 Hz; each pair is printed in ms and in samples. One hop is 5 ms.

FLDIGI AT 61b97f41 (.run-unit/fldigi, src/cw_rtty/cw.cxx unless named)
- Seed. The constructor sets two_dots from the operator's speed, and the spike threshold at a quarter of
  it (317-324). init() resets the tracking filter and runs it once with 2 x cw_send_dot_length, so the
  16-slot average is filled with the operator's speed before any mark arrives (272-273). A change of
  speed or of tracking resets the filter and two_dots to the send speed (481-487).
- Averaging. Cmovavg (src/filters/filters.cxx:273-291) fills every slot with its first value only when
  empty, and after that each value replaces the oldest of TRACKING_FILTER_SIZE = 16 (src/include/cw.h:70).
  Once seeded, one pair moves two_dots by a sixteenth of its difference from the average.
  cw_receive_speed = KWPM / (two_dots / 2) (501-502).
- Spikes. cw_noise_spike_threshold = cw_receive_dot_length / 2 (515). A key-down shorter than that is
  dropped before it can pair, and it does not become last_element (818-822). Because of the seed, this
  is live from the first mark.
- Pair acceptance. The current element must be more than 2 and less than 4 times the last, or the
  reverse (832-843). update_tracking refuses a pair whose lengths are more than 4 apart, either element
  under KWPM/200 (6 ms), or its dah over 3 x KWPM/5 (720 ms) (524-531), then runs the filter (533).
  last_element is every non-spike element, across letter gaps too (844).
- Classing. A dot is anything up to two_dots, a dash anything longer (846-855). update_tracking does not
  ask how either element of a pair was classed.

WHAT 6a0b65a1 DOES DIFFERENTLY, from its own code
- The average is rebuilt from nothing on every read, over one 12 s window. It is filled with the
  window's FIRST pair, which then holds 16 - (pairs - 1) of the 16 slots at the end. No seed is taken.
- So until the first pair arrives the spike rule is off. After it, the threshold is half that pair's
  dot.
- Its pair test, its bounds and its average step are fldigi's.

THE DIFFERENCE, AS A MECHANISM
- PAIR ACCEPTANCE does not separate them. Every pair in the broken windows passes fldigi's own test:
  2 to 4 times apart, over 6 ms, dah under 720 ms (cw.cxx:524-531, 832-843). So there is no pair this
  rule accepts that fldigi's would not. The pairs that pull the broken reads slow put a mark of 300 to
  470 ms as their long element: 220/455, 80/305, 150/320, 130/305, 160/435, 180/445, 190/445. At the
  key's 61 ms that is five to seven and a half units, longer than any single element. One such pair
  also sits in a helped read (031838 r52, 210/470), outvoted there by 24 others. Those marks come from our trigger joining
  elements across a short key-up, upstream of the rule. fldigi's update_tracking would take them too,
  because it does not ask how either element was classed.
- A COUNT, or the fill that stands in for fldigi's seed, does not separate them either. This is where
  6a0b65a1 departs from fldigi: the window's first pair fills the average where fldigi's seed does, and
  it keeps 16 - (n - 1) slots.
  - It amplifies the merged first pairs of 031948 (11-12 slots) and 031905 (9-14 slots).
  - It amplifies the first pairs of the helped reads just as much: 031838 r4-r19 hold 7-13 slots of
    65/220, and 032113 r43-r56 hold 8-10 slots of 60-85/180-215.
  - 004507's E and 032050's reads 49 and 52 broke with 0 or 1 slots held, where the fill is gone and
    only the pairs themselves decide.
  A rule that seeded the average as fldigi does would therefore undo part of 031838's help along with
  031948 and 031905. It would leave 004507 and 032050's later reads as they are.
- The same pairs, at comparable counts, move helped and broken windows alike. What differs is how many of
  a window's marks are joined elements (032050's later reads, 031948, 031905), and in 004507's case a
  Farnsworth sender read nearer its own speed breaking a letter the faster path had read right. Neither
  is a mechanism of the pair rule. A limit on either would be a constant picked from these seven
  recordings, which the instruction forbids.

VERDICT: NO MECHANISM OF THE PAIR RULE SEPARATES THE HELPED WINDOWS FROM THE BROKEN ONES. No second
form is registered. Task 3 is not run, and unit 460 counts as step 2's first of three units with no
kept change (2.4's count 0 to 1).

For the next author, a finding and not a rule: the broken moves rest on marks of five units or more
that our trigger makes by joining elements. fldigi's own tracker gives such a mark one sixteenth of a
vote because it is continuous and seeded, not because it refuses it.
