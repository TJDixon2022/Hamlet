== 4. THE JOIN

One place: TheRequirementsAreMeasuredTests.Measure(name, set, condition, kind, settled, scores) (:120-135), the
call our text enters. BothDecodersAreScoredAlikeTests hands it the port's emissions as CwCharacter, mapped by
BothDecodersAreScoredAlikeTests.Mapped (458 DECIDED (2)): " " -> word gap; `*` -> placeholder (Confidence
Unreadable, text MorseAlphabet.Unreadable, so the locator treats it as it treats ours); every other string ->
itself at Confidence High (sure). At = InputSample / 8000. The scores come from the same per-recording rule
(KeyedRecordings[i].Score or SyntheticCq.Whole) applied to the port's own reading.
What it lacks: nothing the scorer reads. The port has no span-level evidence (SpanLogLikelihoodRatio is NaN)
and no confidence; neither enters the four metrics. One property of the scorer, not of the join: Within locates
each stretch independently, so on a text far from its keys two stretches can land on overlapping characters
(the port on cw-2026-09-24-004322, stretches 1 to 3, below). It is the rule ours is scored by, applied unchanged.

== 5. LATENCY (one synthetic case, cq-18wpm-15db)

Our characters come out of the settled pass about 1.27 s after the character's last mark ends (median over the
21 read sure and right); the port's about 0.30 s after (median over 18). No span edge rests on a time, so this
difference cannot move a stretch; it is reported so a reader does not mistake it for a decoding difference.

== 6. THE PITCH EACH DECODER IS GIVEN

Ours starts at 600 Hz and tracks its own; the figure is the median of CwToneTracker.ToneHz over the hops on
which HasMeasuredPitch was true ("none" where it never measured one). The port is given the median of
CwPitchInstrument's keyed windows over the whole file (as WhatTheSecondDecoderReadsFirstTests did, unit 456),
synthetic cases included, never the construction pitch (615 Hz with 3 Hz of drift) and never our tracker's.
Every recording had at least 5 keyed windows, so the port was run on all 35.
