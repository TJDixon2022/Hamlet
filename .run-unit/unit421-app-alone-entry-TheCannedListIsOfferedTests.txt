and then EITHER a "macro" naming one of Hamlet's own three - answer, report, confirm -

and the hand-back behind, exactly as a line you type on a card is framed -

the rest. A "macro" line is the macro Hamlet already composes, unchanged.

in. Hamlet never writes either file, never creates your copy and never repairs it. If
your copy cannot be read, the menu says so and offers nothing rather than guessing.
