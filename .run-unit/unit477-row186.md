| 2026-09-28 | **A keyed signal is a run of bars; the tracker obeys the meter** (R91 and criterion 12.4 in `PHASE_PLAN.md`). A keyed CW signal is detected as a run of flat-topped bars in a bin - level held within a small tolerance for at least a dit, dropped, held again - at any pitch the filter passes and at any loudness; noise is what never holds a level. The envelope detector's floor and margin are removed; when the meter finds keying at a pitch the tracker mixes there at once, and its keying flag follows the meter's verdict rather than the 20 dB swing gate.
